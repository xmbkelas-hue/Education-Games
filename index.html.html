✦ Mission 01 • Decode the text

Should Schools Limit Smartphone Use?

Read the passage. Pay attention to the writer's position, reasons, and suggested action.

Should Schools Limit Smartphone Use?

[1] Schools should limit students' smartphone use during learning hours. Although smartphones can provide quick access to information, unrestricted use often distracts students from lessons and reduces meaningful interaction in the classroom.

[2] First, notifications and social media can interrupt students' concentration. Even a short distraction may make it difficult to follow an explanation or complete a complex task. As a result, students may miss important information.

[3] Second, excessive phone use can reduce face-to-face communication. Classroom discussions help students explain their ideas, listen to different opinions, and develop social skills. If students focus on their screens, these opportunities may be lost.

[4] However, banning phones completely is not always necessary. Teachers can allow them for specific learning activities, such as checking reliable sources or using educational applications, when the purpose is clear.

[5] Therefore, schools should establish clear smartphone rules: phones should be kept away during explanations and discussions, but may be used when teachers explicitly include them in a learning task. This balanced policy can protect students' focus while preserving technology's educational benefits.

🔎 Analyst's quick guide

Paragraph 1 introduces the writer's position. Paragraphs 2–4 develop reasons and qualification. Paragraph 5 proposes what schools should do.
